Geometry SnacksBite size problems... made interactive!

Geometry Snacks and More Geometry Snacks are two books of bite size geometry puzzles by Ed Southall and Vincent Pantaloni, published by Tarquin. Each puzzle fits on a page with its figure, and each has more than one way to solve it.

Here a selection of the puzzles is redrawn as Live Geometry drawings. Drag the white points to experiment! The solutions are in the books.

All puzzles

Every puzzle here is Ed Southall and Vincent Pantaloni's, from their books Geometry Snacks and More Geometry Snacks, published by Tarquin. Only some of the puzzles are here, with no solutions: the books have all of them, each solved more than one way.

The drawings are redrawn for Live Geometry, a free and open source geometry website, with the authors' permission. Live Geometry is not affiliated with the authors or the publisher. Live Geometry is mostly hand-written by Kirill Osenkov, but AI assistance was used to modernize it. The interactive drawings are free to use and MIT licensed.
