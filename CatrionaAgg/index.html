Since 2018 Catriona Agg, a maths teacher, has shared a geometry puzzle every few days: a diagram drawn in felt tip and a single question. Here each diagram is redrawn as a live construction. Drag the white points and watch what changes, and what doesn't. The answers aren't here: Andrew Stacey has catalogued every puzzle, and gathered the techniques and solutions, at Mathforge Notes.

Every puzzle here is Catriona Agg's: her question, her diagram, her colors. Follow her on BlueSky for new ones; her puzzles of 2018 are collected in the book Geometry Puzzles in Felt Tip.

The catalogue, the topics and the links to techniques and solutions come from Andrew Stacey's Mathforge Notes, where every puzzle has its own page with the original drawing.

The drawings are redrawn by hand for Live Geometry, a free and open source geometry website, which is not affiliated with either of them. If a drawing is wrong, or if you are Catriona and would like anything changed, please open an issue.
